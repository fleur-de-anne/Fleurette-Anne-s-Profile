ABOUT ME:

   I am a Bicolana but currently residing somewhere in Quezon City, NCR. I am a 2nd year student in BAMS and an artist that can draw, animate and do arts & crafts!

   I enrolled in COMM10 to understand human interactions in spaces where communication is vital. I wish to incorporate my little knowledge of graphic design and arts into this type of information sharing especially with today's digital age. I want it to be as effective as possible so that the audience can actually learn and use the knowledge without overwhelming their minds. Hoping that one day, I can contribute to society's education and growth for a possibility of a brighter future for all.



THINGS I LOVE:

Arts & Crafts

  These are the hobbies that I most cherished and have gladly spent my time on. It taught me many valuable things like patience and acceptance that I heavily correlate to my everyday life. I learned to be precise in crocheting, clay modeling and sewing, as well as being a creative mess in drawing and painting. Despite the difficulties I encountered for years of mastering the skills (even today), I enjoy just as much. It showed me another world that I harmonize with and be able to be a part of the community that is also dedicated to arts.


Digital Media

   Just like the arts, the media has been part of my life. It is actually the reason why I started to pursue arts in the first place. It inspired me to replicate the wonderful things that I saw in multiple types of entertainment media. Whether it be a video game, a movie film, an animated short, or a documentary, emotions were drawn and I want to share the awe-strucking experience that eventually led me to loving art as a whole and become the person I am today.
